


Based on OIAI’s current positioning and the Bhubaneswar AI-training market, I recommend
treating the new website as a lead-generation + education + AI-career platform, rather than
just an institute brochure. OIAI already presents AI, Generative AI, Cloud AI, AI Agents,
projects, internships, certifications and multiple learning formats, so the redesign should organize
these into a much clearer conversion journey.
